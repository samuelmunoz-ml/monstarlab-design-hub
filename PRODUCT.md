# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Design team members, new hires, and cross-functional partners at Monstarlab Americas. They use the hub during onboarding, before starting a new project, or when they need to reference standards mid-work. Context: a designer at their desk — focused, professional, short on time. They need answers fast, not exploration.

## Product Purpose

A single-page internal design hub that centralises Monstarlab's design system, process guidelines, onboarding materials, and working agreements. Success means a new hire can self-onboard, a PM can understand what design needs from them, and a senior designer can find any token or guideline in under ten seconds.

## Brand Personality

Bold, precise, borderless. The brand is confident without being loud, dark without being cold, technical without being sterile. Monstarlab is a global agency that takes craft seriously.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dark dashboards (Notion-dark, Linear clones)
- Neon-on-black crypto/gaming aesthetic
- Overly corporate "Enterprise blue" feel
- Anything that looks like a Bootstrap admin template
- Soft pastel "design-tool" aesthetics (Figma-style lavender/pink)

## Design Principles

1. **Precision over decoration** — Every element earns its place. No gradients, shadows, or motion that doesn't communicate something.
2. **Dark with warmth** — The palette is deep navy, not cold black. Yellow as accent gives warmth without brightness.
3. **Information first** — Typography and hierarchy carry the page. Components support reading, never compete with it.
4. **Consistent confidence** — Same visual weight for the same conceptual weight, everywhere. No orphaned patterns.
5. **Craft that teaches** — A design hub should model what it preaches. The quality of this interface IS a design standard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. All interactive elements need visible focus indicators. Reduced motion must be respected. Yellow (#FFFF00) on deep navy background passes contrast at large text sizes; use cautiously at small sizes.
